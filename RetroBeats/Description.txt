  RetroBeats is a 2D "fighting" rhythm game where the player has to time their inputs to the characters' movements. The game was made as a school project with the brief  of "minimal UI rhythm game", with a team of 7 others artists and designers where I was the only programer. This being the first rhythm game that any of us ever made led us down a two and a half months iteration journey. We scraped and reiterated through several concepts until we managed to land on the current iteration, which we later polished into the final product.

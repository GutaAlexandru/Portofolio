  CyberBreak is a 2D puzzle platformer created during a 48 hour game jam with the theme "everything is broken",where I was the sole programer of the team. The core mechanic of the game is the "build" mechanic, where the player has to fill in missing parts of the level with a limited pool of platforms, which they then must beat. My main takeaway from this project was learning to adopt an "it's good enough" mindset, that you often don't have the time to polish mechanic to perfection (doubly so during a game jam).

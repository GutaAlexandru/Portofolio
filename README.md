## RetroBeats
RetroBeats is a 2D "fighting" rhythm game where the player has to time their inputs to the characters' movements. The game was made as a school project in Unity with the brief of "minimal UI rhythm game", with a team of 7 others artists and designers where I was the only programmer. 

This being the first rhythm game that any of us ever made led us down a two and a half months long iteration journey. We scraped, iterated and reiterated through *several* concepts, from shrinking shadows around the characters, idea inspired by [OSU](https://osu.ppy.sh/) , to color changes in the characters' hues to signal their intent. Another huge roadblock for the project was making the animations flow well between idle, attacking, hit etc. states as nearly half of the assets were already finished by the time we had settled on our final concept, and there was no discussion of making anything but small adjustments because of time constraints. This, combined with my at the time limited knowledge of the Unity animator component, made integrating reliable visual ques while keeping the animations short to allow for tight timings into a real challenge.

Overall, this project got me really accustomed to working back and forth with members of the team from other departments as we tried to find the best solution by combining our different perspectives and skillsets.

## CyberBreak

  CyberBreak is a 2D puzzle platformer created during a 48 hour game jam with the theme "everything is broken", where I was the sole programmer of the team. The core mechanic of the game is the "build" mechanic. The player has to fill in missing parts of the level with a limited pool of platforms, which they have to then complete. My main takeaway from this project was learning to adopt an "it's good enough" mindset, that you often don't have the time to polish mechanic to perfection (doubly so during a game jam)

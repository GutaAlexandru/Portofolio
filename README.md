## RetroBeats
RetroBeats is a 2D "fighting" rhythm game where the player has to time their inputs to the characters' movements. The game was made as a school project mad in Unity with the brief of "minimal UI rhythm game", with a team of 7 others artists and designers where I was the only programmer. 

This being the first rhythm game that any of us ever made led us down a two and a half months long iteration journey. We scraped, iterated and reiterated through *several* concepts, from shrinking shadows around the characters, inspired by [OSU](https://osu.ppy.sh/), to ~~color based~~. Another huge roadblock for the project was making the animations flow well between idle, attacking, hit etc. states as nearly half of the assets where already finished by the time we had settled on our final concept, and there was no discussion of making anything but small adjustments because of time constraints. This, combined with my at the time limited knowledge of the Unity animator component, made integrating reliable visual ques while keeping the animations short to allow for tight timings into a real challenge.
